usage: lean-pkg receipt [-h] {slurm,systemd} ...

positional arguments:
  {slurm,systemd}
    slurm          a lower-tier receipt from a finished Slurm job, its verify log and its stdout
                   seal (--template: an sbatch wrapper that meets it; --check: preflight the job's
                   files)
    systemd        a lower-tier receipt from a finished systemd --user service unit (run bounded
                   with --run, or its saved properties), its verify log and its stdout seal

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
