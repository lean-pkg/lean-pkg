usage: lean-pkg formalization [-h] {render,anchor,kept} ...

positional arguments:
  {render,anchor,kept}
    render              render formalization.yaml (v0.4, what Palomar reads) from the registry and
                        the repo at HEAD; stdout unless --write/--out (`formalization NAME` is the
                        same)
    anchor              bind the companion to one posted arXiv version (writes package files,
                        never the registry)
    kept                the canonical kept view of formalization.yaml (derived keys removed) and
                        its SHA-256, for a release-input audit; --against REV: exit 1 when a kept
                        key changed (read-only)

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
