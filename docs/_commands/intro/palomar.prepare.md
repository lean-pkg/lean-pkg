| Form | What it does | Key flags |
|---|---|---|
| `palomar prepare NAME` | build a Palomar submission bundle; no network request, nothing submitted. Default directory `<checkout>/.lean-receipts/palomar-<HEAD12>/` (`--out DIR` elsewhere; an existing non-empty target is refused, never overwritten): `formalization.yaml` (the renderer's output, schema-valid; placeholders are warnings), `configs/<path>` (every comparator configuration tracked at HEAD, byte-identical, and the [headline configuration](../comparator.md#headline-configuration-the-papers-main-results) when one is declared; README.txt names it for the main-results entry and its `record` command; JSON `headline_config`), `receipts/<file>` (the recorded gate and comparator receipts, validated against HEAD: bound to it or to an evidence-only ancestor), `README.txt` (the manual steps, the Palomar URLs and the `record ... palomar-submitted` command), `MANIFEST.sha256` (`sha256  path` per file; `sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256`). Refused (exit 1, nothing written, `--dry-run` included) without either receipt or when one is not bound to HEAD, without consent, when the render fails the schema, when `palomar check` finds any `error` gap at HEAD (each listed as `✗  CODE: detail` with its fix; `warn` gaps never refuse and print as `lean-pkg: warning: palomar check: ...`), or on blocking `public-safety` hits in the bundle (scanned like a fresh-history export). Records `palomar-prepared` {commit, bundle, bundle_sha256 (sha256 of MANIFEST.sha256)}. `--dry-run` runs every check and prints the plan, writing nothing. `--json`: {ok, name, dry_run, bundle, commit, configs[], files[], bundle_sha256, placeholders[], public_safety {block_hits, warn_hits}, palomar_check {ready, commit, errors, warns, codes[], command}, manuscript_findings, warnings[], event}; `manuscript_findings` is the claims ledger's summary of what the formalization found in the manuscript (one text line when there is one; not in the bundle, never a refusal) | `[--out DIR]` `[--dry-run]` |
