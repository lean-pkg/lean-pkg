| Form | What it does | Key flags |
|---|---|---|
| `claims rebase NAME --from-tex OLD.tex --to-tex NEW.tex --to-aux NEW.aux` | move `claims.yaml` and `coverage.yaml` (committed at `HEAD`) to a new manuscript version (#6); never writes the registry or the checkout, never changes a claim status, never sets `fixed_in`. Refused (exit 1): `OLD.tex`'s SHA-256 differs from the manuscript `sha256` `coverage.yaml` records, or is none of the `.tex` hashes of the source-baseline record of its arXiv version (`formalization anchor`; none recorded: `--from-sha256` must state it; the proposal drops `manuscript.eprint_sha256`), TeX that is not UTF-8 or has unbalanced environments, a referenced label defined twice in `OLD.tex`, `NEW.tex` or `NEW.aux`. Each referenced label (coverage `paper.label`, else `paper.number` via `--from-aux`; a finding's `location.label`; a label in a `citation`) is classified by its statement or equation environment, compared after a fixed normalization: `unchanged` (carried; number, page and line re-keyed from `NEW.aux`), `changed`, `missing`, `moved-into-proof`, `not-in-old`, `unsupported`, `unnumbered`; all but `unchanged` need review and stay as they were. `--out-dir DIR` (new or empty) gets the proposed `claims.yaml` and `coverage.yaml`, `rebase-report.json` (`claims-rebase/1`) and `.md` with old/new excerpts, `review/` excerpts and diffs, and `MANIFEST.sha256`; deterministic; without it nothing is written. Exit 0 all carried, 1 review needed or refused, 2 usage; `--json` {ok, counts, labels[], rows[], findings[], citations[], needs_review[], proposed_errors[], files[], ...}.
